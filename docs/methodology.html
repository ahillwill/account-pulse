How it works

Account Pulse gives every account a health score from 0 to 100 every morning. Rules do the math so the score is explainable; an AI agent then reviews the evidence and makes the final call.

The grading scale

0–44 · CriticalSerious risk to the renewal or relationship. Act this week.
45–64 · WatchWarning signs. Check in soon.
65–100 · HealthyStable or growing. 80+ often means expansion potential.

Every account starts at 70 (healthy, nothing notable). Risks lower the score and opportunities raise it. Overlapping risks are capped at −55 in total, because one event (like a layoff) often shows up in the news, an SEC filing, and the job board at once.

What the numbers mean

  • Score
    0 to 100. Higher is healthier.
  • 1-day and 7-day change
    Points gained or lost since yesterday and a week ago.
  • Momentum
    Improving if up 5+ points over 7 days, Declining if down 5+, otherwise Stable.
  • Status since
    The first day of the account's current status.
  • ARR not healthy
    Annual contract value of all accounts in Watch or Critical.

The AI agent

For each account, a Claude agent starts from the rule score, decides which evidence to check (hiring, news, filings, CRM, score history), and submits a status, a reason, and one next action. It may override the rules only with evidence, and it may only use facts the data returned. If it's unavailable, the rule score is used.
